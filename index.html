Welcome to TrainXplor

TrainXplor is a centralized learning hub designed to help you master the processes in your current role and explore workflows across other departments. The site combines structured learning paths with practical, on-the-job practice so you can build confidence and competence quickly.

What you will find:

  • Training videos that demonstrate step-by-step processes and real-world scenarios.
  • Interactive quizzes to check understanding and reinforce key concepts.
  • Supporting documents such as checklists, SOPs, and quick-reference guides for day-to-day use.
  • Hands-on practice modules and simulations that let you apply skills in a safe environment.

By combining multimedia instruction with assessment and practice, TrainXplor prepares you to perform confidently in your current role and gives you the tools to cross-train into other teams.
